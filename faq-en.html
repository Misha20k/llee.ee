FAQ

Faults and diagnostics

01Do you take small household electrical jobs?

Yes. You can contact us about a single faulty socket, switch, light fitting, breaker or thermostat. Small jobs for private clients are a normal part of our work.

02What should I do if a breaker keeps tripping?

Disconnect appliances on that circuit where possible. If the breaker trips again, there is a burning smell or visible damage, do not keep resetting it. Send a photo of the panel and describe when the fault occurs. We diagnose the cause instead of replacing the breaker blindly.

03A socket is hot, crackling, sparking or smells burnt — is it urgent?

Yes. Stop using the socket. If it is safe to do so, switch off the relevant circuit breaker. Do not dismantle an energised socket. If there is smoke or fire, leave the dangerous area and call 112.

04Why are some sockets or lights not working?

The cause may be a breaker, switch, connection, junction box, neutral conductor or damaged cable. The exact fault location is found by inspection and measurements. First tell us what stopped working and what happened before the fault appeared.

05Do you repair electric underfloor heating?

Yes. We check the supply, thermostat, temperature sensor, connections and heating loop. After diagnostics we explain which part is faulty and what repair options are possible. If the damage is under the floor finish, the solution depends on its location and type.

06Can you find intermittent or hidden electrical faults?

Yes, although intermittent faults may require testing while the problem is present or more than one stage. We compare the symptoms with visual inspection, electrical measurements and thermal imaging where appropriate.

07Do you inspect wiring after water damage?

Yes. After safe isolation and with regard to the actual condition of the premises, we inspect accessible parts, electrical equipment and insulation resistance where technically possible. By agreement, we can issue an act or technical assessment reflecting the condition on the inspection date.

08Can old wiring be checked before buying or renovating a property?

Yes. We perform a technical inspection of the accessible installation and electrical panel, together with agreed measurements. The result describes the condition of the checked parts and helps prioritise further work.

FAQ

Electrical panels, protection and measurements

09What is included in an electrical panel inspection?

We check accessible connections, device ratings and condition, circuit distribution, signs of overheating, protective devices and the arrangement of N and PE conductors. The exact measurements depend on the installation and the client’s task.

10Do you check earthing and the PE protective conductor?

Yes. We inspect accessible connections and perform measurements suitable for the particular installation. Unsafe links inside sockets are not a proper substitute for a protective conductor.

11Can you test an RCD or RCBO?

Yes. Where technically possible, we check the connection, trip current and trip time with dedicated equipment. Before installing or replacing a protective device, we assess the circuit design, wiring condition and intended load.

12What instruments do you use for diagnostics?

We use a Fluke 1664 FC multifunction installation tester and other professional instruments selected for the task. Thermal imaging is used where appropriate. The test set depends on the fault — no single instrument replaces engineering judgement.

13Can I receive an invoice, work act or measurement results?

Yes. Depending on the service, we can issue an invoice, a work completion act, recorded measurement results or a technical assessment. The required document format should be agreed before work begins.

16Can I send a photo of the fault first?

Yes, that is the best way to start. Message Dmitri on WhatsApp at +372 8127 1119 or through Messenger and send an overview, a close-up and a photo of the electrical panel if it may be related.

17What should I include in the first message?

Include the town or address, what is not working, when the problem started and what has already been tried. Add 2–3 clear photos. Do not open energised parts or dismantle equipment just to take a photo.

18Can you quote from photos?

Photos sometimes allow us to estimate the likely scope and provide preliminary information. The exact cost of diagnostics and repair depends on the fault cause, access to the wiring, required materials and measurements. Additional work is priced and agreed before it is carried out.

19What happens during a diagnostic visit?

We first clarify the symptoms, inspect accessible parts and perform the necessary checks. We then explain the identified cause, possible risks and further options. Work beyond the agreed diagnostics is carried out after approval.

20Will the electricity need to be switched off?

Some tests and repairs require isolating the whole installation or a single circuit. We will explain this on site. If there are clear danger signs, switch off the affected circuit beforehand only if it can be done safely.
